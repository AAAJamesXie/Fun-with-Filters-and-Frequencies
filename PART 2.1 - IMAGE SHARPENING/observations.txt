For Taj Mahal, alpha = 0.5 and 1 make the marble, dome and arches clearer. Alpha = 2 looks stronger, while alpha = 4 adds obvious bright and dark outlines. I choose alpha = 1 as a moderate example. On the cat photo, alpha = 0.5 and 1 make the eyes, fur and scratching-post texture clearer. Alpha = 2 looks stronger, but alpha = 4 makes the fur rough and adds bright outlines around the face and ears. I keep alpha = 1 for the main result. In the blur test, alpha = 2 improves the eyes and face outline, but fine fur and whiskers remain much softer than the reference. The PSNR improves from 29.377 to 31.788 dB, but the original detail is not fully recovered.
