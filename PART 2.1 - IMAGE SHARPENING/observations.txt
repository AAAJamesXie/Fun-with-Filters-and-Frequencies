For Taj Mahal, alpha = 0.5 and 1 make the marble, dome and arches clearer. Alpha = 2 looks stronger, while alpha = 4 adds obvious bright and dark outlines. I choose alpha = 1 as a moderate example. On the selfie, sharpening makes the glasses and hair clearer but also makes skin texture and image marks stronger. Alpha = 4 looks too harsh. In the blur test, sharpening improves the glasses and mouth, but fine hair and face details are still missing. A better PSNR does not mean the original detail is fully recovered.
